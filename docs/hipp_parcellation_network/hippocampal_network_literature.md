# Hippocampal SMN, DMN and attention-network organization in prior studies

The maintained HCP cross-fit workflow obtains three anterior-to-posterior hippocampal clusters from connectivity gradients and uses their cortical connectivity profiles to display them as anterior/VAN, middle/SMN and posterior/DMN. This is a K=3 analysis and the network names describe the selected profile-level association, not proof of exclusive network membership. See [cross-fit template matching](crossfit_template_matching.md).

## Evidence closest to the current result

- **Ezama et al., 2021, European Journal of Neuroscience**, HCP 7T, 172 participants: hippocampus-restricted spatial ICA and whole-brain dual regression identified an anterior hotspot whose connectivity map correlated with the Yeo somatomotor network, and a middle-to-posterior hotspot associated with the default network. This supports SMN anterior to DMN and a posterior DMN-related region, though its SMN hotspot is anterior rather than specifically middle and it did not identify an anterior VAN hotspot. The reported map correlations were 0.49 with SMN and 0.56 with DMN; correlations with VAN were 0.00 and 0.03, respectively. These are map correlations, not the strength of the current study's FC. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1111/ejn.15213); local parsed text: [Ezama](../../refs/Ezama_2021_Hippocampus_Subfields_RSFC/Ezama_2021_Hippocampus_Subfields_RSFC.md).
- **DeKraker et al., 2025, Nature Methods**, HippoMaps: a multimodal hippocampal resource reports strong neocortical DMN connectivity, especially in subiculum, and organization across both longitudinal and transverse axes. This is background support for hippocampal DMN connectivity, not the three network labels or their anterior-to-posterior order. [Article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-025-02783-3); [local parsed text](../../refs/DeKraker_2025_HippoMaps/DeKraker_2025_HippoMaps.md).

## Important contrasting evidence

- **Vos de Wael et al., 2018, PNAS**: in hippocampal surface gradients, anterior regions were relatively more connected to default, limbic and somatomotor cortex, whereas posterior regions were more connected to visual and dorsal/ventral attention networks. This explicitly addresses ventral attention but its relative anterior-posterior trend is opposite to the present network naming. [Article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6176604/).
- **Katsumi et al., 2023, Communications Biology**: large HCP discovery and independent GSP validation datasets showed relatively stronger attention connectivity posteriorly, DMN connectivity in middle/anterior portions, and somatomotor connectivity at the anterior extreme. These are continuous gradient relationships, not mutually exclusive parcels. The paper is also in Zotero (EV6FZQ32). [Article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10097701/).
- **Zheng et al., 2021, PNAS**: individual precision mapping related the head and body to DMN and the tail to the parietal memory network (PMN); the article also reports a winner-take-all comparison against all cortical networks. The study is in Zotero (PARD5JPS). PMN should not simply be relabeled as the present Kong/Yeo VAN. [Article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8403906/).
- **Angeli et al., 2025, PNAS**, intensive individual sampling and replication: reports an anterior default-network and posterior salience/parietal-memory-network preference. The authors also examined voxelwise network preferences without requiring all voxels to be assigned to one of two networks. Their network taxonomy is not automatically identical to Kong/Yeo VAN. [Article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11760929/).
- **Kember et al., 2026, Nature Communications**, 471 participants aged 5–21: individually mapped anterior and body systems were associated with different default subnetworks; the posterior system showed preferential connectivity to Yeo-17 control-c/medial-parietal memory and salience-related networks. This is a developmental cohort, not a direct replication of adult HCP results. In Zotero (TFVWFXCB) and [local parsed text](../../refs/Kember_2026_Hippocampus_Longitudinal_Axis/Kember_2026_Hippocampus_Longitudinal_Axis.md). [Article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-74572-1).
- **Peng et al., 2023**, three-cluster hippocampal parcellation: the left anterior cluster had the most positive default connectivity and most negative ventral-attention connectivity; this contrast did not generalize to the right. Direction of correlation matters, and this result does not demonstrate an anterior VAN-preferring parcel. In Zotero (IXR5FDC9). [Article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10216165/).

## Interpretation

Previous work supports hippocampal participation in default and somatomotor networks and heterogeneous cortical connectivity along its long axis. Ezama et al. offer the closest partial spatial match: SMN anterior to a middle/posterior DMN hotspot. The literature surveyed does not establish the full anterior VAN → middle SMN → posterior DMN order; several influential studies report attention-related connectivity toward the posterior end and default-related connectivity more anteriorly. Distinct surface/volume representations, samples, cortical taxonomies, network scoring, competitive assignment and gradient versus discrete clustering can contribute to differences, but no specific cause has been established for this project. An informative direct comparison would place both studies' cortical network definitions and unthresholded hippocampal connectivity profiles in a common space; the result would show whether the disagreement reflects label definitions or different connectivity maps.
