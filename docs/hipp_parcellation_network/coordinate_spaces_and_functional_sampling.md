## Answer to the cross-project comparison

The official sources do not establish `corobl -> ACPC/T1w -> MNI` as one identical pipeline shared by all three projects. HippUnfold supplies the corobl-to-input-anatomy step. HippoMaps demonstrates transforming anatomical surfaces to the functional volume's frame, called `space-func` in its examples. Kember's published analysis notebook does not implement the image-registration and BOLD-sampling stages. For the local HCP implementation, the proposed MNI destination follows the selected BOLD volume, and ACPC describes that HCP anatomical input. The common-space principle is supported; the exact HCP transform chain, warp convention and interpolation remain specific implementation choices whose evidence must come from those inputs and commands.

## Official HippUnfold findings

### Coronal-oblique space is supported, but is not the only output space

HippUnfold's `corobl` space is its coronal-oblique anatomical processing frame. The documented preprocessing registers an anatomical image to the template, concatenates the template-to-coronal-oblique transformation, and defines cropped hippocampal images there. Cropping and upsampling change the grid; the registration and coronal-oblique transformation also change the anatomical frame. Therefore `corobl` must not be assumed to coincide with an input T1w or an MNI volume's physical coordinate frame. [Current preprocessing documentation, lines 44–51](https://github.com/khanlab/hippunfold/blob/5fa82bb09803a7bb5faa16d87b6a987c45746f1f/docs/pipeline/pipeline.md#L44-L51)

The output documentation supports anatomical surfaces in `T1w` and `corobl`, plus unfolded surfaces in `unfold`. `cropT1w` is a high-resolution cropped anatomical image output, distinct from `corobl`. Corresponding surface vertices permit a sampled metric to be displayed on another representation of the same topology. This vertex correspondence does not make the representations' physical coordinates interchangeable during volume sampling. [Current output documentation, lines 30–71](https://github.com/khanlab/hippunfold/blob/5fa82bb09803a7bb5faa16d87b6a987c45746f1f/docs/outputs/output_files.md#L30-L71)

Supported output spaces and default retained outputs must be distinguished. `--output_spaces` defaults to an empty list; the Snakefile automatically adds the input anatomical modality (`T1w` or `T2w`) to `ref_spaces`. For the T1w route, retaining corobl as an additional formal output requires requesting it explicitly. The `dsegtissue` and `hippb500` routes also select corobl. Surface targets use these `ref_spaces` plus a separate unfolded midthickness surface. Thus the T1w workflow does return anatomical-space surfaces; it does not default to corobl-only output, nor does it always retain both T1w and corobl. [CLI configuration, lines 203–209](https://github.com/khanlab/hippunfold/blob/5fa82bb09803a7bb5faa16d87b6a987c45746f1f/hippunfold/config/snakebids.yml#L203-L209); [space selection, lines 21–36](https://github.com/khanlab/hippunfold/blob/5fa82bb09803a7bb5faa16d87b6a987c45746f1f/hippunfold/workflow/Snakefile#L21-L36); [surface targets, lines 163–198](https://github.com/khanlab/hippunfold/blob/5fa82bb09803a7bb5faa16d87b6a987c45746f1f/hippunfold/workflow/rules/common.smk#L163-L198)

### Upstream sampling explicitly pairs matching spaces

In `sample_myelin_map_surf`, HippUnfold pairs a `space-corobl` T1w/T2w ratio volume with `space-corobl` midthickness, inner, and outer surfaces. It then invokes Workbench volume-to-surface mapping with ribbon constraints. This is direct official evidence that sampling in corobl is valid when the sampled volume is in that same frame. It is a myelin example, not an upstream endorsement of sampling an independently registered MNI BOLD volume with unchanged corobl points. [Current mapping implementation, lines 43–102](https://github.com/khanlab/hippunfold/blob/5fa82bb09803a7bb5faa16d87b6a987c45746f1f/hippunfold/workflow/rules/myelin_map.smk#L43-L102)

### Surface transformations are applied to vertices

The surface generator obtains the corobl coordinate image's NIfTI affine, transforms the extracted mesh into image physical coordinates, then writes the points to GIFTI. The writer stores those points in the GIFTI point array without explicitly adding a separate inter-space registration transform. One should not assume that selecting a corobl GIFTI instructs a later sampler to perform T1w or MNI registration automatically. [Surface generator, lines 16–22](https://github.com/khanlab/hippunfold/blob/5fa82bb09803a7bb5faa16d87b6a987c45746f1f/hippunfold/workflow/scripts/gen_isosurface.py#L16-L22); [mesh-to-image transformation, lines 89–93](https://github.com/khanlab/hippunfold/blob/5fa82bb09803a7bb5faa16d87b6a987c45746f1f/hippunfold/workflow/scripts/gen_isosurface.py#L89-L93); [GIFTI writer, lines 51–74](https://github.com/khanlab/hippunfold/blob/5fa82bb09803a7bb5faa16d87b6a987c45746f1f/hippunfold/workflow/lib/surface.py#L51-L74)

Upstream `affine_gii_corobl_to_orig` reads a corobl surface and the matrix named `desc-affine_from-{modality}_to-corobl_type-ras_xfm.txt`, then calls `wb_command -surface-apply-affine` directly to produce a T1w/T2w surface. It does not request inversion in this command. Consequently the filename alone does not establish the direction of a matrix when applied to points; matching upstream usage and checking actual source/target geometry are necessary. This rule returns surfaces to the input anatomical frame. It does not apply the HCP `acpc_dc2standard` transform or supply an HCP MNI BOLD sampling step. `T1w` describes the input image's frame; ACPC is a property of the particular HCP input, not a universal HippUnfold T1w convention. [Current surface transformation, lines 344–383](https://github.com/khanlab/hippunfold/blob/5fa82bb09803a7bb5faa16d87b6a987c45746f1f/hippunfold/workflow/rules/native_resampling.smk#L344-L383)

The transform-generation rules compose the anatomical/template and template/corobl ITK transforms, and separately produce ordinary RAS and inverse-RAS variants. Ordinary RAS conversion does not invoke `-inv`; the explicitly inverse variant does. This explains why the transform name must not be treated as an instruction to invert it for surface points. [Current transform composition and conversion, lines 104–210](https://github.com/khanlab/hippunfold/blob/5fa82bb09803a7bb5faa16d87b6a987c45746f1f/hippunfold/workflow/rules/warps.smk#L104-L210)

## Official HippoMaps functional mapping

The current official 3T resting-state tutorial starts from a `space-T1w` midthickness surface, applies two affine transformations and a warp to move its points into `space-func`, and samples a `space-func` functional volume using Workbench's `-enclosing` method. It then smooths the sampled metric using the original T1w surface. Its introduction explicitly describes the surface-to-functional-space transformation and identifies this as dataset-specific example code. This demonstrates that sampling coordinates and smoothing geometry can differ while retaining vertex correspondence. It is not the literal HCP `ACPC/T1w -> MNI` prescription, and its interpolation also differs from a trilinear sampler. [3T tutorial explanation, lines 103–107](https://github.com/MICA-MNI/hippomaps/blob/eebd4fe82e69f32e793ac691b8346e71780a757a/tutorials/MRI-3T-rsfMRI.ipynb#L103-L107); [3T transformations, sampling and smoothing, lines 117–173](https://github.com/MICA-MNI/hippomaps/blob/eebd4fe82e69f32e793ac691b8346e71780a757a/tutorials/MRI-3T-rsfMRI.ipynb#L117-L173)

The additional 7T resting-state notebook also transforms a T1w surface through two affines and a warp into `space-func` and samples a `space-func` volume with `-enclosing`. Unlike the 3T notebook, its smoothing command uses the deformed functional-space surface. Neither notebook uses an unchanged corobl surface to sample an MNI BOLD volume. [7T transformations and sampling, lines 106–154](https://github.com/MICA-MNI/hippomaps/blob/eebd4fe82e69f32e793ac691b8346e71780a757a/tutorials/extras/MRI-7T-rsfMRI.ipynb#L106-L154); [7T smoothing, lines 156–163](https://github.com/MICA-MNI/hippomaps/blob/eebd4fe82e69f32e793ac691b8346e71780a757a/tutorials/extras/MRI-7T-rsfMRI.ipynb#L156-L163)

The current stable `v2.2.0` adds inner/outer registration controls, model changes, Pixi dependency management and cluster resources. ## HCP functional sampling

HCP sampling starts from the subject's native T1w midthickness surfaces.
Workbench converts the declared FNIRT field to a world displacement field;
the inverse solver maps the T1w vertices into the MNI BOLD frame with a
0.001 mm residual tolerance. The transformed surfaces are used for trilinear
BOLD sampling, while native T1w geometry is used for smoothing.

Each subject's `surface_sampling_summary.json` records the native T1w source,
the generated MNI sampling surfaces and the registration input. Mapping a
sampled metric onto a corobl display surface preserves vertex correspondence;
it does not resample the BOLD volume. The NIfTI coverage rule does not use a
CIFTI hippocampal contour as an exclusion mask.

The implementation is maintained in
[`surface_coordinates.py`](../../scripts/core_workflow/lib/surface_coordinates.py)
and [`surface_sampling.py`](../../scripts/core_workflow/lib/surface_sampling.py).
